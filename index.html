//// diii

Live-code iii devices from the browser: a REPL, the scripts on the device, an editor with the device's API, and a view of the device you can play.

over USB, in Chrome, Edge or Opera

devices

achene A fully customizable performance MIDI controller: 12 encoders, 16 RGB keys and a 2.42" OLED. diii shows its panel live and plays it. Open Grid A DIY 16×8 grid running iii. diii mirrors its LEDs and presses its keys, and updates its firmware. monome grid and arc monome's own iii devices, with their stock firmware: the REPL, files, editor and reference all work.

start

  • iii docs: what iii is and how scripts work
  • : every function, offline too
  • drop a .lua file anywhere to upload it; type help() once connected

diii is a fork of monome's web-diii (GPL-3.0), with device views and tools by vfmod.
